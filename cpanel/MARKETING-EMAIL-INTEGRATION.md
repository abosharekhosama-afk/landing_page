# Email Marketing UI Integration

Integrated into the REQUIRED + RELATED Employee 4 build:
- Complete email marketing campaign workspace UI.
- Campaign wizard: details, templates, recipients, review and scheduling.
- Drag/drop-style email builder UI with blocks, properties and preview.
- Campaign preview and test-email UI (local/mock only).
- Automation UI components.
- Marketing mock data and local service layer required by the UI.
- Email marketing styles and builder styles, including RTL/responsive rules.

The local service is intentionally non-sending and non-persistent; it does not use OAuth, API keys, or a real email provider.
